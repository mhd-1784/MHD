For our October Monthly Club Social, we were delighted to welcome the Nautical Archaeological Society (NAS) to the Welwyn Sports & Social Club for a talk on the workings of the NAS.

Mark Beattie-Edwards, CEO and Sara Hasan, long-time member of NAS and Secretary of the NAS Diving Club drove all the way from Portsmouth for the evening to present.

![NAS Talk](https://res.cloudinary.com/h2zix6yq/image/upload/v1785484667/mhd/articles/img_20241030_210232109.jpg)

![Presenters](https://res.cloudinary.com/h2zix6yq/image/upload/v1785484668/mhd/articles/img_20241030_210302641.jpg)

![Audience](https://res.cloudinary.com/h2zix6yq/image/upload/v1785484668/mhd/articles/img_20241030_210448126.jpg)

Mark told us how the NAS operates and Sara explained how she had became involved many years ago and now was one of the few Trustees of a UK Protected Wreck.

Mark and Sara also explained how non-NAS divers are welcome to join expeditions and help out with surveys and other on-site work on wrecks around the UK.

The talk was well attended and very well received by club attendees - many of whom expressed interest to get involved.

To that end, the club committee is considering taking up Mark's offer of running a Taster Session on a pool night over winter and then looking towards running open water courses on the NAS sunken wreck in Stoney Cove and even a protected wreck day trip organised just for our club next year...
