Sodwana Bay is a protected marine region of the iSimangaliso Wetland Park, Africa's first UNESCO

World Heritage Site. And it shows, with a profusion of fantastic marine life on shallow reefs in

excellent condition!

.

On the dives we admired a profusion of Butterflyfish, Anthias, Angel Fish, Trumpet Fish, Box Fish, Blue

Spot Stingrays and other critters plus plentiful large Grouper and shoals of Jacks which are usually

fished out at other locations. Also feisty Leopard Eels of great size, some taking an unhealthy

interest in the divers! The reefs were mainly quite shallow so hour-plus diving was the norm. We

also jumped in with wild dolphins at one point and (on one dive) heard humpback whale calls.

.

We stayed at Siso Dwana Lodge (https://www.sisodwana.com/) who provided very comfortable

accommodation near to the village and operated three large RHIBs from which the diving was done.

This involved some impressively skilful skippering, threading through the breakers on the way out

and then beaching the entire boat complete with divers and gear after the diving!

.

At the end of the week we had a 2-day trip over the border into Mozambique, at the beach resort of

Ponta do Ouro. We did a couple of dives on which we saw White Tip and Bull Sharks, though due to

an adverse ocean current perhaps not as many as usual. The resort itself was however delightful

with a truly magnificent beach, perfect surf and a relaxed vibe. It was also the place where we

sampled the infamous R&R Cocktail at Fernando's bar (served in half litre glasses and consisting

largely of the local rum!)

.

On the final day our hosts took us out to the Hluhluwe–Umfolozi Game Reserve where we had a

fantastic day admiring Rhinos, Elephants, Giraffe, Warthogs, Buffalo, Wildebeest, Nyala, Warthogs and

Hippos! Truly one to remember.

Many thanks to Andrew and Sue of Siso Dwana Lodge who were fantastic hosts and really went the

extra mile with the diving, accommodation and excursions. Highly recommended.

.

Finally, thanks also to James Pemberton for organising: if anyone wants to give this a rinse and

repeat he will be happy to advise.
