Friday 4th April saw 20 of us descend on the Welwyn Sports & Social club for the annual quiz night. The turnout was somewhat lower than last year, but is likely a result of us clashing with the Easter break, also changing the date, and holding it quite a bit later on in the calendar than we did last year.

Regardless, great fun (and cheap drinks) were had and after several rounds hosted by our quizmaster (thanks Clive!), the 'Under Pressure' team proved they were appropriately named and took the prize pot. Well done guys (though we all hope to beat you next year!).
