Being fully into the lovely late Spring/early Summer public holiday season, we felt it would be a great idea to take advantage of the warming waters, improving weather, and the extended slot at Arlesey to host a BBQ.

![In the water](https://res.cloudinary.com/h2zix6yq/image/upload/v1785484547/mhd/articles/2025-05-05-arlesey-bbq/2025-05-05-d-img_0937.jpg)

![More in the water](https://res.cloudinary.com/h2zix6yq/image/upload/v1785484548/mhd/articles/2025-05-05-arlesey-bbq/2025-05-05-h-img_0956.jpg)

With over 20 people confirmed in advance, and shopping done, we headed to Arlesey to get some diving in as well as post-dive refreshments and food. As usual with an extended Arlesey slot, the club took full advantage in getting some training in including me successfully completing my Open Water Rescue module (thanks David M) to complete my Sports Dive qualification.

Delicious burgers and hot dogs were cooked on the braie for our hungry divers and partners by our resident chef Tracey, and despite the weather being fairly overcast and a bit windy, we had a lovely time both in and out of the water.
